Teach a job by hand

1
Hold the arm, release the motors

The arm goes limp - keep a hand on it.

2
Move to the start and record

Position the arm where the job begins, then start recording and move it through the whole job.

3
Grab / release (optional)

uArm: suction on / off. SO-100: gripper close / open. Hold still when you click.

4
Save the job

Hold the arm - the motors lock again when you save or cancel.

Tips

• Move calmly - the robot repeats your exact speed.
• Pause briefly at important poses (they are measured on replay).
• Finish at a safe pose: that is the robot's "home" after the job.
• Teach Arm 2 separately, or convert Arm 1 jobs on the command line (copy-jobs).
